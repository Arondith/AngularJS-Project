Built for explainability

A small system with clear responsibilities.

SharePlate uses AngularJS for application state and interaction, while keeping persistence, matching logic, and presentation concerns separated.

01

Controller

Coordinates form state, filtering, metrics, and user actions.

02

Storage factory

03

Matching service

Scores compatible records with transparent business rules.

04

Filters & directives
